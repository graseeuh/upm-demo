Join Our team

At United Property Management, we are committed to providing a workplace where team members feel supported, valued, and empowered to grow professionally. We believe investing in our people creates a stronger company culture, exceptional service, and long-term success. Our comprehensive benefits package is designed to support the health, wellbeing, career development, and work life balance of our team members.

At UPM, we believe our team members are our greatest asset. We are committed to creating opportunities for professional growth and advancement of our team at every stage of their careers by investing in nationally recognized training programs and professional organizations. Our culture is built is built on continuous learning, leadership development, and a strong commitment to promoting within.

Our investment in training extends far beyond onboarding. Through ongoing education, mentorship, and career development opportunities, we equip our team members with the skills and knowledge needed to excel in their current roles while preparing them for future growth within the organization. We have partnered with the Disney Institute, Dale Carnegie, Grace Hill, the National Apartment Association, and the Society for Human Resource Management to make sure we stay prepared and up-to-date. UPM values talent, recognizes achievement, and is dedicated to assisting team members build successful and rewarding long-term careers.

United Property Management Benefits

  • Compensation

    Competitive compensation and employee recognition that celebrate individual and team achievements.

  • Health & Wellness

    United Property Management team members enjoy comprehensive medical, dental, vision, and wellness benefits that support overall well-being.

  • Financial Benefits

    Financial security is supported through life insurance, disability coverage, and a 401(k) retirement savings plan.

  • Work-Life Balance

    At United Property Management, we support a healthy work-life balance through paid-time off, holidays, and family leave benefits, including paid maternity & paternity leave.

  • Growth & Development

    At United Property Management, we believe that investing in our people is investing in our future and are committed to helping our team members grow, succeed, and build meaningful, long-term careers.

  • Life at UPM

    A collaborative and philanthropic culture built on teamwork, recognition, and meaningful connections helps our team members thrive.

Tuition Reimbursement Program (UPMCER)

United Property Management offers the UPMCER Tuition Reimbursement Program to encourage team members to further their education, expand their knowledge, and pursue their professional goals.

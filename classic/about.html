Our story

The UPM family is proud of its contributions to South Florida's housing and continues to strive for excellence by “Paying Attention to Every Detail.”

United Property Management is one of South Florida's largest, privately held management firms, managing over 9,000 apartment homes. We strive to provide affordable luxury housing to meet the needs of South Florida's growing population.

In 1962, architect, developer, and entrepreneur José Milton made Miami, Florida, his home. After several successful projects, he founded United Property Management in 1976. Today, UPM owns, manages, and maintains rental communities throughout South Florida.

Condominium & Commercial Developments

Development & Construction.

As a vertically integrated company, J. Milton & Associates handles land acquisition, planning, construction, and sales and marketing in-house. Every project undergoes rigorous value engineering, with senior management closely involved at every stage. This hands-on approach and attention to detail ensure the delivery of high-quality communities that consistently exceed the company's own standards.

At its core, the company believes renters at every price point deserve modern technology, quality craftsmanship, comfortable amenities, and thoughtful community design. Each development reflects a commitment to aesthetics, functional floor plans, security, and convenience.

Privately held and family-owned for over 50 years, J. Milton & Associates has remained true to its founding principles. Today, the family brings a modern perspective while upholding the company's longstanding commitment to quality and integrity.
